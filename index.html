Personal Info

Researcher in energy storage materials and battery characterization

I am a battery-materials researcher working on high-energy-density batteries, synchrotron X-ray characterization, machine learning, and battery modeling. My current affiliation is the Department of Chemical Engineering at Tsinghua University.

Education: PhD in Materials Science and Nanoengineering at Rice University; MS in Applied Physics and Applied Mathematics at Columbia University; BEng in the College of Engineering at Peking University. Research experience includes postdoctoral work in Chemical Engineering at Tsinghua University beginning September 1, 2025, with projects spanning solid-state batteries, X-ray imaging, electrode reaction heterogeneity, and data-driven materials analysis.

Research Ideas

Questions I am exploring

01

High energy density batteries

Study solid-state and composite electrolytes, high-voltage cathode interfaces, and thick electrode configurations.

02

Operando X-ray characterization

Use synchrotron and X-ray methods to observe reaction heterogeneity, zinc growth, particle cracking, and mesoscale battery-material evolution.

03

Machine learning for materials, devices, and X-ray techniques

Combine physics-informed machine learning, statistical analysis, and battery modeling to extract richer signals from complex characterization data.
